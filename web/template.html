    Method and limits

    For each dimension, 100 points means identical to the target site and 0 points means as unlike it as the least similar land on Earth (2nd to 98th percentile, weighted by area). The total is the weighted mean over the dimensions that have both a target value and a value for the cell. Where the target lies outside what Earth offers, no cell reaches 100 points. That is intended: for temperature and air pressure the number shows how far the analogy holds.

    Data

    • Moon terrain: LOLA topography. Mars terrain: MOLA spherical harmonic model MarsTopo719, flattening removed. Both are limited to the same 18.5 km wavelength as the Earth data, and slope and relief are computed by the same code.
    • Earth temperature and day-night swing: NASA MODIS Terra and Aqua land surface temperature (MOD11C3, MYD11C3), 2003 to 2024.
    • Earth vegetation: NASA MODIS NDVI (MOD13C2), 2003 to 2024.
    • Earth albedo: NASA MODIS MCD43C3, shortwave, snow-free. Apparent thermal inertia is (1 − albedo) divided by the day-night swing, used only where the swing exceeds 5 K.
    • Earth precipitation, air pressure and dust optical depth: NASA MERRA-2, 2003 to 2024, about 55 km resolution.
    • Filters: NASA MODIS land cover (MCD12C1) for water, forest, cropland, cities and permanent ice.
    • Earth terrain: ETOPO1 (NOAA), 10 arcminutes. This is the one input that is not a NASA or ESA product; the fine pipeline replaces it with the Copernicus DEM.
    • Sun elevation from latitude and axial tilt. Ground ice is an estimate from mean surface temperature. Place names: Natural Earth.

    What this map cannot do

    • Cells are about 37 km wide and the terrain baseline is 18.5 km. That describes landscape types, not landing sites.
    • Albedo, thermal inertia and dust are measured with different instruments on each body. Their target values are rough estimates until measured TES, OMEGA and Diviner values replace them, and they should then be compared by rank.
    • The lunar temperature target is a rough estimate. Because nothing on Earth comes close, it changes the score but not the order.
    • Mineralogy and the subsurface, such as lava tubes, are not included.
    • Gravity, vacuum, radiation and the thin CO₂ atmosphere cannot be reproduced anywhere on Earth.
